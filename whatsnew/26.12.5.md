## Make it yours

- **Light and dark, your way**: follow your system's appearance and pick a separate theme for each mode. Plus two new themes, **Opaline** and **Prism**.
- **Sidebar zoom** replaces interface size and density, and on web and desktop you can **drag to resize** the room and member lists.
- **Show DMs in Home** if you'd rather have everything in one list.

## Get around faster

- **Download and copy media** straight from the message menu.
- A new **help center** is one tap away in the header.

## Never miss a call

- **Android rings for calls** even when the app is closed or the phone is locked.
- **Set a backup call server** if your homeserver doesn't provide one.
