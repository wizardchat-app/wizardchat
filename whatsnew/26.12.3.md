## Make some noise

- **Soundboard in calls**: play applause, crickets, a drumroll and more for everyone in the call, or add your own sound packs to a room or space.
- **Calls on iPhone and Android, redesigned**: a FaceTime-style view for one-on-one calls, switch between front and back camera, and push-to-talk.

## Find your way around

- **A Threads tab in the inbox** shows new replies in threads you're part of.
- **Jump to a date** in any chat.
- **See a message's edit history**, and edit the captions of photos, videos and files after sending.

## Make it yours

- **Pronouns and an emoji for your status**, all in one new profile editor. Profiles also show the rooms you share.
- **Build your own theme** with the new theme editor.
- **Streamer mode** hides emails, session details and ignored users.
