## Sound better, see more

- **A noise filter for calls**: pick Enhanced in Voice & Video settings to filter out typing, fans and traffic on your device. It's experimental, so tell us how it sounds.
- **Picture-in-picture on iPhone and Android**: keep a call going in a small window while you use other apps.
- **Watch Together playlists**: line up videos for everyone and see who's watching.

## Feel at home

- **Jump back in** on the mobile home tab gets you to your most recent chats in one tap, and **Active now** shows which voice rooms your friends are in.
- **Mark rooms and DMs as unread** to come back to them later, and turn on **message previews** in the room list if you like seeing the latest message.
- **Nicknames per room and space**, so you can be someone different in every community.

## Little bits of magic

- Moderators get an **audit log** in room and space settings.
- Photos and videos **load and send faster**.
